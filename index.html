HYZR PARTNERS Refer & Earn Program

Earn up to ₹5000 by referring your friends to join HYZR!

We are excited to introduce our Refer a Friend Program, designed to reward you for helping us grow our HYZR partner community. Here’s how it works:

How You Earn

When you refer a friend to become a HYZR Partner (driver), you will receive bonuses based on your friend’s activity within their first 30 days.

Condition Trips Completed Bonus You Earn
Within 7 days 25 trips ₹1000
Within 15 days 50 trips ₹1500
Within 30 days 100 trips ₹2500
Total Possible Earnings – ₹5000

Example

👉 If your referred friend completes:
  • 25 trips in 7 days → You earn ₹1000
  • 50 trips in 15 days → You earn an additional ₹1500 (Total ₹2500 so far)
  • 100 trips in 30 days → You earn another ₹2500 (Total ₹5000)

Rules & Conditions

  • The bonus is only applicable if your referred friend joins HYZR as a Partner using your referral.
  • Trips completed must be valid HYZR trips. Cancelled trips will not be counted.
  • The referral bonus will be credited directly to your HYZR account.
  • Maximum reward per referral = ₹5000.
  • HYZR reserves the right to amend or withdraw this offer at any time.
